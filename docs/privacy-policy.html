Privacy Policy for AyahReel

Last updated: 24 July 2026

AyahReel ("the app") lets you create short Quran recitation videos on your phone. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

In short: AyahReel does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, or have access to any personal information about you. We operate no servers and hold no user data. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user profiles.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your phone. It never leaves your device, and we cannot see it:

  • Reels you create — the video files, plus the reciter, surah and verse range used to make them.
  • Photos or videos you choose as a reel background. These are read only when you select them, used only to render your reel, and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • App settings — reminder preferences and sharing options.
  • Your streak and points — a local count of reels you have created and shared.
  • Cached Quran text, recitation audio and word-timing data — downloaded once so the app works offline and loads faster.

All of this is removed when you uninstall the app. You can also clear the cached downloads at any time from Settings → Storage, and delete individual reels from My Reels.

Photo and media access

If you choose your own photo or video as a reel background, the app asks your device for access to that file. Access is used solely to render your reel on your device. If you save a finished reel, it is written to your device's gallery in an album named "Ayah Reels". Nothing is uploaded.

Network connections

The app connects to the following third-party services purely to download Quran content. No personal information, account identifier, or advertising identifier is sent with these requests — only the standard technical information any internet request carries, such as your IP address, which those services handle under their own policies:

  • alquran.cloud — Quran text and English translation. alquran.cloud
  • EveryAyah.com — recitation audio files. everyayah.com
  • GitHub — a one-time download of open word-timing data from the quran-align project, used to highlight words in time with the recitation.

All content is downloaded over encrypted HTTPS connections and cached on your device, so repeat use requires no further requests.

Notifications

The app can send you a daily reminder to create or read a reel. These are local notifications, scheduled entirely on your device — no push server is involved and no notification token is created or shared. They are optional: you are only asked for permission after you choose to enable a reminder, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your device settings.

Sharing your reels

When you share a reel, the app hands the video file to your device's share sheet, or directly to WhatsApp if you use that option. What happens next is governed by the app you share to and by the privacy policy of that service. We are not involved in the transfer and receive no copy.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no tracking or profiling technology. We do not use cookies, device fingerprinting, or advertising identifiers, and we do not share or sell any data — because we hold none.

Children's privacy

AyahReel is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children because we do not collect data at all.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. All data created by the app lives on your device and is entirely under your control: delete individual reels in the app, clear cached downloads in Settings → Storage, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what data it handles, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above revised before that version is released.
